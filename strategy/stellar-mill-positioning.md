# Stellar Mill: organized business, informed tax decisions

## User direction

Prioritize higher-earning business owners and more complex business tax situations. Sell the transformation from uncertainty and scattered business records to organization, visibility and informed decisions. Address owners who already have a tax preparer. Use tax preparation as an entry point to appropriate Stellar Mill business services. Keep one separate family-focused post; it must not dilute the business-owner campaign. Do not use derogatory audience labels in public copy. This replaces the earlier broad personal-tax/refund emphasis.

## Positioning hypothesis

Help business owners move from tax-time scrambling to organized records and a clearer next step for taxes and business operations. Lead with control and relief, then explain the mechanism: records, bookkeeping setup, appropriate professional tax review, and planning. Avoid guaranteed tax reductions, invented savings, fake testimonials, or implying every expense is deductible.

Higher revenue is not identical to profit or tax liability. No revenue cutoff, service pricing, savings amount or client outcome has been provided. Qualify fit rather than inventing those details. Neither research nor competitor longevity establishes conversion performance; test cost per qualified booked consultation and downstream customer value.

## Research collected

- Federal Reserve Banks, 2025 Report on Employer Firms: reports on 2024 survey results. Rising costs were cited by 75% of employer firms, paying operating expenses by 56%, uneven cash flows by 51%. These are broad small-employer findings, not a survey specifically of affluent tax clients. https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2025/2025-report-on-employer-firms
- IRS recordkeeping: good records help monitor business progress, prepare financial statements, identify income, track deductible expenses and prepare/support tax returns. https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping
- IRS estimated taxes: taxes generally must be paid as income is earned; many business owners need estimated payments; underpayment/late payment can lead to penalties. This does not mean all clients must make the same payments. https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes
- IRS business-expense resource guide: deductions and limitations are topic-specific. Eligibility and records must be reviewed; no automatic universal write-off or S-corp savings promise. https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/deducting-business-expenses
- Stellar Mill Business Builder, https://bb.stellarmill.com/: public client bundle /assets/index-BoF0uDX_.js contains service names Business Formation, Registered Agent Service, Business Records Package, Bookkeeping Setup, Payroll Setup, Contractor Onboarding, Tax Planning, Funding Readiness Review, Government Contracting Setup, Compliance & Annual Review. Listed service names do not verify delivery scope, prices, credentials, turnaround, recurring bookkeeping, financing approval, or client outcomes.
- Existing checkup: https://checkup.stellarmill.com/#checkup. Free 60-second readiness/routing tool; requests contact information before results. It is not a document review, tax computation or filing service.

## Emotional transformation and concrete mechanism

| Current concern (hypothesis to validate) | Desired state | Service discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales look strong but the financial picture feels unclear | Know income, expenses and what records are missing | Records and bookkeeping setup |
| Tax decisions happen only at filing time | Understand estimates and questions before filing | Appropriate tax review and planning |
| More staff/contractors increase admin work | Clear responsibilities and organized records | Payroll setup and contractor onboarding |
| Already has a preparer but wants broader business support | Understand what support is already covered and what is missing | Fit review; don't disparage the existing preparer |

These are messaging hypotheses, not fabricated customer interviews or testimonials.

## First business-owner static tests

### A. Organization and emotional relief

Headline: **Know what's earned. What's owed. What's next.**
Primary text: A growing business brings more decisions—not just more paperwork. Start with Stellar Mill's free Tax Checkup to find an appropriate tax-service path. Contact details requested to view results.
Visual: an uncluttered, premium business desk; three large cards INCOME / RECORDS / NEXT STEPS. Cream, charcoal and gold. Do not show invented bank balances, savings, approval badges, readiness scores or before/after financial results.
Message after the click: clarify existing recordkeeping, the main tax concern and whether a professional consultation is appropriate.

### B. The existing-preparer objection

Headline: **Already have a tax preparer? What's covered between returns?**
Primary text: Filing, bookkeeping setup and tax planning meet different needs. Stellar Mill's free Tax Checkup is a starting point for identifying the right tax-service conversation. Contact details requested to view results.
Visual: a calendar motif with FILE as one milestone and RECORDS / ESTIMATES / PLANNING as discussion topics. Do not imply an existing preparer is incompetent or that Stellar Mill already provides a verified recurring package. Complementary coordination must be confirmed operationally before promising it.

### C. Tax planning without a refund gimmick

Headline: **The tax conversation shouldn't start at the deadline.**
Primary text: Organized records make it easier to review income, expenses and tax questions with a professional. Take Stellar Mill's free Tax Checkup and find your next step. Contact details requested to view results.
Visual: calm owner-workspace scene with one clear calendar and organized folders. Do not promise a refund, zero tax, a specific tax reduction, or a guaranteed result from the quiz.

### One separate family post

Headline: **More clarity for the family. Less tax-time guesswork.**
Primary text: Income forms, family changes and eligibility questions deserve a careful review. Start with Stellar Mill's free Tax Checkup to find your next step. Contact details requested to view results.
Audience language: parents and families. Avoid gender stereotypes, assumptions about dependents or benefits, and guaranteed EITC/Child Tax Credit/refund amounts. Keep in a separate organic series or separately planned ad set so results are not mixed with premium business acquisition.

## Funnel design

Current available entry point: business-owner ad → existing Tax Checkup → relevant service route → professional consultation/intake.

Recommended next landing-page iteration: a clearly labeled business-specific path that asks about business type, whether books are current, revenue band separately from profit, existing preparer, and primary objective. Collect only necessary nonsensitive qualification information. Do not collect tax returns, Social Security numbers or bank details in an ad lead form. Announce a paid/free review only after Stellar Mill confirms delivery scope and terms.

After a real fit assessment, offer the appropriate listed business service. Do not force a new-business formation funnel on an established high-earning owner or advertise funding access as approval.

## What counts as conversion evidence

Choose a primary event: qualified booked business consultation, with a documented qualification rule. Track completed checkups and bookings separately, connect downstream attendance, service purchase, revenue and retention where available. Link clicks and refunds are not the success metric for the business-services funnel. Configure consent-aware conversion measurement before optimizing on that event.

Test one distinct message per creative while keeping the actual offer, audience and destination comparable. No budget, lead cost target or customer acquisition ceiling has been authorized. Do not launch or change spend based on this strategy document alone.
